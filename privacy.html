Privacy Policy — Ghafwa

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Ghafwa runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. We do not collect any personal data and operate no servers that receive your data.

Location is used on-device only, to find your nearest station and follow your trip so the alarm rings before your stop. It is never sent to us or stored.

Place search and maps are provided by Apple Maps. Subscriptions are processed by the App Store; we never see your payment details.
